Academic Assessment Framework Scope

Understanding MetaAOT Findings & Boundaries

This tool implements a Shift-Left static analysis framework to assess Maven dependency readiness for GraalVM Native Image prior to compilation.

✓ What MetaAOT Provides:
  • Early, lightweight detection of structural metadata evidence in seconds.
  • Identification of embedded native configurations, reachability specs, and missing gaps.
  • Prevention of costly trial-and-error compilation cycles.
⚠ Research Limitation & Boundary:

Structural evidence alone does not guarantee native compilation success. Dynamic runtime behaviors, reflection, and internal GraalVM reachability analysis remain outside the scope of static structural inspection.
